[ISAMORE](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3779212.3790162) (ASPLOS 2026) combines e-graph anti-unification with pattern vectorization to discover semantically reusable, vectorized custom instructions. [ScaleISA](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3845814.3850048) (ASPLOS 2027) uses multi-level e-graph anti-unification to jointly discover instructions and explore their microarchitectures under resource budgets. Together, they automate instruction finding toward APS's goal of generating specialized processors and software stacks from domain applications.
