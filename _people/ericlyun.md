Yun (Eric) Liang leads the APS project and is an Endowed Boya Distinguished Professor at Peking University, with appointments in the School of Integrated Circuit and School of EECS. His research spans electronic design automation, hardware-software co-design, and computer architecture, developing algorithms, programming models, and tools for high-performance, energy-efficient computing. He is an ACM Distinguished Scientist and was inducted into the MICRO Hall of Fame in 2026; his group's ISAMORE work received the ASPLOS 2026 Best Paper Award. See his [homepage](https://ericlyun.me/) for his full biography and research activities.
